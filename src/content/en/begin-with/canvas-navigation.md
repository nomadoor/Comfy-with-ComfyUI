## Moving the Canvas

Move freely around the vast canvas.

- **Left drag on empty space**
- **Drag with middle button (wheel click)**
- **Left drag while holding Space key**

> If you left drag on a node, the node itself moves instead of the canvas.<br>
> In places where nodes are dense, moving using the **Middle Button** or **Space Key** is stress-free and recommended.

## Zoom In / Out

- Rotate mouse wheel
- Click `+` / `-` buttons at the bottom right of the window

## Fit View / Zoom to Node

Convenient when you get lost or want to focus on a specific node.

- **Show All**: Click `⏹️ (Fit View)` at the bottom right of the window with nothing selected
- **Zoom to Node**: Click `⏹️ (Fit View)` with a node selected

![](/media/begin-with/canvas-navigation/legacy_gyazo_076543f8a710ae811112a54812adb348.mp4){media=loop}

## Minimap

You can check the overall picture of the canvas in a small window.

- Click the `🗺️ (View Minimap)` icon at the bottom right of the window
- Or shortcut key `Alt + M`
