## Moving the Canvas

Move freely around the huge canvas.

- **Left-drag on empty space**
- **Drag with the middle button (press the wheel)**
- **Left-drag while holding the Space key**

> [!TIP]
> If you left-drag on a node, the node itself moves instead of the canvas.<br>
> Where nodes are packed together, moving with the **middle button** or the **Space key** is much less frustrating.

## Zoom In / Out

- Scroll the mouse wheel
- Click the `+` / `-` buttons at the bottom right of the window

![](/media/begin-with/canvas-navigation/legacy_gyazo_e4a9566bd88e073497c6e75b02b7bbf9.mp4){media=loop}

## Fit View / Zoom to Node

Handy when you get lost or want to focus on a specific node.

- **Show everything**: Click `⏹️ (Fit View)` at the bottom right of the window with nothing selected
- **Zoom to Node**: Click `⏹️ (Fit View)` with a node selected

![](/media/begin-with/canvas-navigation/legacy_gyazo_076543f8a710ae811112a54812adb348.mp4){media=loop}

## Minimap

See an overview of the whole canvas in a small window.

- Click the `🗺️ (View Minimap)` icon at the bottom right of the window
- Or press the shortcut `Alt + M`
